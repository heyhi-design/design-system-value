# Sources

Every precedent named in the kit, with a public URL. Precedents are cited as examples of a pattern, not as benchmarks; where a source's numbers appear in the kit they carry the provenance grade the field guide assigns (A controlled study; B first-person with method; C vendor, analyst, or self-report; D unsourced; X do not cite). Several of the Medium and eightshapes.com pages refuse automated fetches and were read from secondary summaries; they are marked (s).
